⚠️ 重要規則 Important Rules
⚠️ 將軍 Check
當你的棋子可以吃掉對方國王，就叫「將軍」！被將軍的那方必須馬上保護國王。國王格子會變成紅色！
When your piece can capture the enemy King next move, that's "Check"! The King's square turns RED. You MUST protect the King immediately!
🏆 將死 Checkmate = You Win!
被將軍但無論怎麼走都無法逃脫，就叫「將死」！被將死的一方輸了。把對方國王將死是目標！
If the King is in Check and has NO way to escape — that's Checkmate! The player in Checkmate LOSES. This is your goal!
🌟 士兵升級 Pawn Promotion
士兵走到棋盤最遠一排，可以升級成王后、城堡、主教或騎士！通常選王后最強！
A pawn reaching the far end of the board promotes to Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight. Queen is usually the best choice!
🏰 王車易位 Castling
國王和城堡可以同時移動的特殊規則！兩者都沒移動過，且之間沒有其他棋子才可以。可以保護國王！
A special move — the King and Rook swap! Both must not have moved, with no pieces between them. Great for protecting your King!
💡 初學者技巧 Beginner Tips
🎯
控制中央！Control the Center!
中文：把棋子移到棋盤中間，可以攻擊更多地方。
Move pieces toward the middle of the board. From there, you can reach more squares!
👑
保護國王！Protect Your King!
中文：盡早讓國王躲到安全角落，用王車易位。
Castle early to put your King in a safe corner. A safe King is a happy King!
🤔
移棋前先想想！Think Before You Move!
中文：走棋之前先問：我的棋子會被吃嗎？
Before moving, ask yourself: "Can my piece be captured?" Always look for danger!
🐣
讓士兵升級！Promote Your Pawns!
中文：把士兵推向對方陣地，走到底變王后！
Push your pawns forward! If you can make a pawn into a Queen, you're in great shape!
😊
多練習！Keep Practicing!
中文：不要怕輸，每次輸都是學習！
Don't be afraid to lose! Every game teaches you something new. Keep playing!
